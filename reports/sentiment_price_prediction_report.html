Methodology, Baseline Comparisons & Limitations:

1. Strict No-Leakage Time Alignment: Features for predicting bar t+1 are calculated using market and alternative data available strictly at or before the close of bar t.

2. Naive Baseline & Statistical Tests: Model accuracy is benchmarked against a naive persistence baseline (sign of preceding bar return). Binomial p-values are computed to evaluate whether directional accuracy exceeds 50% significantly.

3. Position Tracking: Stated positions reflect retail traders' self-reported holdings in forum discourse. The suggested stance is the quantitative model's risk-adjusted recommendation.

Methodology & Quantitative Model Architecture:

1. Data Ingestion: Multi-source ingestion capturing retail community forums (LIHKG, Futu/Moomoo, Naver Finance, Daum Finance) via Apify actors and institutional news via Exa.com neural search across top financial outlets.

2. Engagement-Weighted Sentiment Index: Weights posts by author reach (logarithmic follower scale), virality (likes, reposts, comments), and exponential time decay (half-life of 3 days).

3. Retail vs Institutional Divergence (RSMD): Computes sentiment delta between retail crowds and institutional coverage to identify potential retail trap distributions near resistance and capitulation accumulation near support.

4. Out-of-Sample Walk-Forward Validation: Machine learning forecasts (blended XGBoost + L2 Ridge Regression) are evaluated strictly on out-of-sample forward folds with zero lookahead bias.
